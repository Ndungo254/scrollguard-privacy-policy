Delete Your Account

ScrollGuard, a product of Zafrionet Limited · Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can delete your ScrollGuard account and its data at any time, directly in the app or by email. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open ScrollGuard and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar (top right of the Home screen) to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your PIN to confirm it is you.
  5. Tap Delete forever. Your account and data are then permanently removed.

Request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, email us from the address on your account:

  • To: zafrionetltd@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my ScrollGuard account
  • Please send it from the email you used to sign in, so we can verify the request.

We will action verified requests and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your profile and settings;
  • the phones you registered;
  • your stolen-phone reports and their location trail and sightings;
  • any evidence photos;
  • your case chats and messages;
  • your push-notification tokens;
  • your sign-in (authentication) account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • After deletion, your data is not retained, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with the law.
  • Evidence photos are in any case deleted automatically within 30 days of capture.
  • Residual copies that may remain in encrypted system backups are cleared within 30 days.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. A phone you had registered will no longer be protected by your account after deletion.
