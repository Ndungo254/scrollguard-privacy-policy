ScrollGuard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ScrollGuard is a phone theft-protection and screen-time app, a product of Zafrionet Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, how it is stored, and the choices you have. We only collect what the app needs to protect your phone and help you manage your screen time.

Summary

The short version

  • We collect your account details, the phones you register, and — only after you report a phone stolen — its location and, optionally, photos and SIM changes.
  • App-usage data used for screen-time limits is read and processed on your phone and is never uploaded to us.
  • Theft data such as location and photos is visible only to you in your account.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address — to create and secure your account (handled by Firebase Authentication).
  • Display name — shown on stolen-phone reports and in case chats.
  • A security PIN — stored only as a salted, one-way hash. We never store the PIN itself.

Phones you register

  • IMEI, phone model and a nickname you choose. Registering a phone lets a buyer check its IMEI before purchase and lets you report it if it is stolen.

Theft-protection data (only after you report a phone stolen)

  • Precise location — when you turn on the Last-location shield, the app records the phone’s location periodically in the background while it is reported stolen, so you can trace it. Collection stops when you mark the phone recovered and never runs before a report.
  • Photos — if you turn on Evidence capture, a single front-camera photo is taken after repeated wrong unlock attempts on a stolen phone. No audio is ever recorded.
  • SIM-change details — the mobile-network operator of a new SIM, if you turn on the SIM-change alert.

Community reports and chat

  • When you report a phone stolen, a report is created that other ScrollGuard users can see. It contains the phone model, a masked IMEI, your display name, and a district-level area (not your exact address).
  • Chat messages between a phone’s owner and someone who finds it. Phone numbers are kept hidden inside the chat.

Technical information

  • Push notification token — to alert you when your stolen phone comes online or someone messages you.
  • On-device app-usage statistics — used to enforce the app limits and schedules you set. This is processed on your phone to show your screen time and is not transmitted to us.

3. How your information is stored and secured

ScrollGuard uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) to store data and run the service. Data is stored on Google’s servers and protected in transit with encryption.

Access is restricted by security rules so that your theft data — locations, evidence photos and private details — can be read only by you while signed in to your account. Evidence photos are kept in a private area that only you can access.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide theft protection: registering phones, checking IMEIs, filing stolen reports, locating, alerting and recovering phones.
  • To provide screen-time features: enforcing the limits, schedules and focus sessions you set.
  • To let owners and finders communicate about a stolen phone.
  • To send you notifications about your phone and your account.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. How your information is shared

  • With Google (Firebase), as our service provider, to operate the app’s backend.
  • With other ScrollGuard users, only the limited public report details described above when you choose to report a phone stolen.
  • When required by law, such as a valid legal request from authorities.

We never share your location, evidence photos, email or PIN with other users.

6. Data retention

  • Evidence photos are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Account and registered-phone data is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, registered phones, reports, chats, location trail and evidence photos are permanently removed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data at any time from the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently erases your data.
  • Turn off any feature or revoke any permission in your phone settings.
  • Access or correct your information, or ask a question, by emailing us at the address below.

8. Acceptable use

ScrollGuard is intended only for use on a phone you own. Do not install it on, or use it to monitor, a device that is not yours. The app stays visible in your launcher and can be removed in Settings.

9. Children

ScrollGuard is not directed at children and is intended for the owner of the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated in the app where appropriate.
